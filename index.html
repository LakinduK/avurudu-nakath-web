Description

Sinhala Avurudu Nakath / සිංහල අලුත් අවුරුදු නැකැත් is your go-to guide for celebrating the Sinhala and Tamil New Year with tradition, timing, and mindfulness.

This app helps you keep track of all the key rituals (චාරිත්‍ර) and their auspicious times/නැකැත් වේලා - such as අලුත් අවුරුදු උදාව, පුණ්‍ය කාලය, ආහාර පිසීම, වැඩ ඇල්ලීම, ගනුදෙනු කිරීම හා ආහාර අනුභවය.

Features:

  • 🕒 Live countdown for each Nakath time
  • 📜 Simple descriptions of Avurudu rituals
  • 🗓 Covers both Sinhala and Tamil traditions
  • 📱 Offline access—no internet needed once data is loaded
  • 🎉 A culturally-rooted experience made for all ages
